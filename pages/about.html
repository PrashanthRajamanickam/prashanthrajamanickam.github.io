My Approach

I don't just document products. I understand how they work.

My technical writing journey has been centered around one principle: documentation should help people accomplish something, not simply describe what a product does.

Over the years, I've worked across API documentation, developer documentation, user guides, online help, knowledge bases, UX content, structured authoring, and documentation strategy.

I work closely with product, engineering, support, and cross-functional teams to understand the technology, identify information gaps, and turn that understanding into useful documentation.

My experience with source code, development workflows, REST APIs, OpenAPI, DITA/XML, Markdown, and Git-based workflows allows me to work comfortably with technical teams and translate engineering concepts into content that users and developers can actually use.
